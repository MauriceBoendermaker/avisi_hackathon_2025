# WattShare — Hackathon Winning Prototype
## (Avisi x ENTRNCE x Energiebank, 2025)

WattShare is the winning solution of the 36-hour “Sociaal Energiedelen” Hackathon held on 2–3 October 2025 at the World Trade Center in Arnhem.  
The hackathon was organized by [Avisi](https://www.avisi.nl/) and [ENTRNCE](https://www.entrnce.com/en/), in collaboration with [Energiebank](https://energiebank.nl/), and challenged teams to design innovative IT solutions enabling households and companies to share excess electricity with vulnerable households that struggle to pay the electricity bills.

We (Maurice Boendermaker, Mathijs de Niet, and Yassine Abderrazik) won first place and a €2000 innovation award for WattShare, a working prototype that combines a smart matching algorithm with a clean, data-driven dashboard.

---

## About the Project 🏆

WattShare is a proof of concept demonstrating how surplus electricity generated by households and businesses can be allocated intelligently to vulnerable households.  
It directly responds to the upcoming Energiewet 2026, which introduces energy sharing in the Netherlands.

### Key Features
- **Custom Python Matching Algorithm**  
  Matches energy donors (households & companies with excess electricity) to vulnerable households based on usage, supply, and fairness rules.

- **React + TypeScript Dashboard**  
  Built with custom React+TS components with the support of Bootstrap (in SCSS) for UI layout and Apache ECharts for data visualization.

- **Realistic Dataset Processing**  
  Powered by anonymized and realistic hackathon dataset from an actual wind turbine and solar panels from 2024.

## Hackathon Context

The challenge addressed a major societal problem:

- 400,000+ Dutch households struggled with energy bills in 2023  
- Estimated to exceed 550,000 in 2025  
- Millions generate more electricity than they consume  
- Energiewet 2026 makes energy sharing possible  
- Key question: **How do we ensure surplus electricity reaches vulnerable households while keeping the system attractive for energy donors?**

**WattShare** demonstrates how a data-driven, algorithmic approach can help.
